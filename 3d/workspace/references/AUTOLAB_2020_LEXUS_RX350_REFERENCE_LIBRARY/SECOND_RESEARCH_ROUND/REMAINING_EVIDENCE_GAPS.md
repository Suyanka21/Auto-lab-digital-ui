# REMAINING_EVIDENCE_GAPS

## Highest-impact gap

The single biggest obstacle is the absence of a VIN-confirmed 2020 RX 350 AWD donor build with owner-approved, multi-angle photography and legitimate access to the applicable technical/service documentation. Without that combination, the research can establish the vehicle family and component relationships but cannot safely resolve exact grade, upholstery, roof, audio, screen, seat or hidden-assembly geometry.

## Component gaps

### Front seats

Missing: one donor’s front/back/side/three-quarter photographs, exact stitch and perforation macro, outboard controls, underside/rail relationship, seat-cover seam map and measured hardpoints.

### Rear seats

Missing: exact five-seat donor photographs from both sides, armrest hinge, belt/buckle geometry, seatback-to-cargo-floor transition, folding angles, tether/LATCH placement and dimensions.

### Dashboard and cockpit

Missing: VIN-specific screen/audio/HUD package, passenger-side dashboard/glovebox close-ups, exact panel-gap transitions, cluster face/button legends and calibrated material captures.

### Centre console

Missing: verified base-AWD close-up from above and both sides, selector/Remote Touchpad geometry, exact cupholder count/retention shape, armrest dimensions and storage depth.

### Door panels

Missing: a complete four-door set from one donor, speaker/audio package, panel cross-sections, clips, stitch spacing, grain, handle geometry and trim continuity.

### Roof/headliner

Missing: roof-option confirmation, headliner/pillar CAD or service diagrams, moonroof opening, overhead console, sunvisor/grab-handle coordinates, clip locations, curtain-airbag interfaces and headliner material code.

### Floor/lower cabin

Missing: bare-cabin photographs, carpet templates, rail bolt spacing, pedal geometry, sill/rocker clips, centre tunnel cross-sections and floor acoustic layers.

### Steering/restraints

Missing: non-F SPORT AWD wheel close-ups, exact button legends, cluster layout, belt routing, buckle shapes, upper anchors, LATCH/ISOFIX coordinates and tether geometry.

## Documentation gaps

Public research did not uncover a complete, openly accessible 2020 RX 350 AWD interior service manual, dimensioned trim drawings or CAD package. Toyota TIS is the legitimate next avenue for repair manuals and wiring diagrams, but access is controlled/subscription-based. OEM parts catalogues are useful for names and branches but are not substitutes for service procedures or dimensions.

## Visual and rights gaps

No collected image is classified as commercial-use permitted. Exact-year galleries exist, but their fetched HTML often exposed labels rather than inspectable pixels. A future pass should obtain permission-cleared donor photography or commission original reference photography.

## Reasonable next research avenues

1. Obtain VIN, window sticker/build sheet and market/grade confirmation.
2. Photograph the actual donor using fixed camera positions for every component.
3. Obtain legitimate TIS or authorized service documentation.
4. Request owner permission for close-up photos of seats, door cards, roof, floor and controls.
5. Capture measured photogrammetry or dimensioned photographs under controlled lighting.
6. Separate factory configuration from optional custom treatments such as starlight headliners.
