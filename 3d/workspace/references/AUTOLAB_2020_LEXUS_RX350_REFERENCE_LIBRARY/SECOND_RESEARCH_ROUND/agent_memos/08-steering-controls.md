# 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD (five-seat) — steering, controls, cluster, vents, belts and anchors

## Exact-year identity and scope

The target is the conventional two-row **2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD**, not the longer RX 350L and not the hybrid RX 450h. Lexus’s 2020 brochure describes the RX 350 as a five-passenger model with a 3.5-liter V6, eight-speed automatic transmission and available all-wheel drive; the same brochure separately identifies the three-row RXL models. [1] The Car Connection’s exact-year gallery labels the relevant photographed configuration “2020 Lexus RX RX 350 AWD” and separately labels RX 450h AWD images. [2] Edmunds lists a 2020 RX 350 AWD trim and five total seats. [3]

Evidence classes used here: **A** = manufacturer/official model-year or product documentation; **B** = established automotive editorial/specification source; **C** = dealer or distributor presentation; **D** = weak/indirect or not independently inspectable. Public availability is not treated as commercial permission.

## What is established

### Steering wheel, spokes and buttons

For the 2020 RX 350 family, Edmunds identifies a tilt-and-telescopic steering wheel with transmission, cruise and audio controls on the wheel. [3] Lexus’s F SPORT model-year page is more specific for the F SPORT variant: both the appearance and Performance packages include a unique steering wheel and shift knob, and the package also changes the meter, aluminum ornamentation and pedals. [4] CarPro’s hands-on review of a 2020 RX 350 F SPORT AWD reports a leather-wrapped F SPORT-badged steering wheel, standard paddle shifters across the lineup, and a bright digital tachometer with a driver-information display on the left. [5]

These sources support the existence and placement category of steering-wheel controls, paddle shifters on the reviewed F SPORT, and the F SPORT-specific wheel treatment. They do **not** establish a single universal button layout for every non-F SPORT AWD trim. The 2020 RX had multiple trims, packages, displays and wheel treatments, so a reference build should keep the steering-wheel button panel and spoke trim configurable unless the target trim/package is fixed.

The official Lexus pressroom also supplies a 2020 RX 350 F SPORT image set, including cabin images. The page identifies the images as 2020 RX 350 F SPORT and links high-resolution originals. [4] The images are useful visual reference for the F SPORT wheel/spoke treatment, but no scale or dimensional information should be inferred from them.

### Instrument cluster / meters

The Car Connection exact-year gallery includes separate image labels for “2020 Lexus RX RX 350 AWD Instrument Cluster” and “2020 Lexus RX RX 350 AWD Steering Wheel,” as well as dashboard, instrument panel, audio system, temperature controls and air-vent images. [2] This is strong exact-year/configuration labeling for visual reference, but the page’s extracted text does not expose the image pixels or identify every button symbol. The CarPro review of a specific 2020 RX 350 F SPORT AWD describes a bright digital tachometer with driver-information display on the left. [5] Lexus’s F SPORT page establishes that the F SPORT package includes a distinct meter. [4]

Do not generalize the F SPORT meter description to every RX 350 AWD trim. Edmunds only establishes standard trip computer, compass and external-temperature display in its 2020 RX 350 equipment listing; it does not provide a complete meter face diagram. [3]

### Air vents and climate controls

The Car Connection gallery labels exact-year RX 350 AWD photographs for “Temperature Controls” and “Air Vents.” [2] CarPro’s 2020 F SPORT AWD review describes a button-heavy center stack for dual-zone climate controls and rear A/C vents, and states that the rear seat has a drop-down console with cupholders. [5] The 2020 brochure describes multi-zone climate control, individual driver/front-passenger temperature settings, available heated/ventilated front seats, and a smog-sensing climate-control system. [1]

The evidence establishes the presence of front climate-control/vent areas and rear-seat A/C venting in the reviewed F SPORT vehicle, but it does not establish that every climate feature or vent finish was standard on every AWD trim. The brochure’s “available” language matters. Do not invent vent dimensions, grille counts, or exact control legends from the text sources.

### Belts, anchors and child-restraint provisions

The exact-year 2020 RX 350 owner-manual URL is linked from the Lexus of Highland Park 2020 model page under “Owners Manual.” [6] The manual PDF itself was not text-renderable through the browser fetcher and returned an access-denied response when direct retrieval was attempted in this session, so no page-level manual claim is made here beyond the existence of the exact-year manual link. [6][7]

The manufacturer brochure identifies the conventional RX as five-passenger and distinguishes it from the RXL’s third-row seating. [1] Edmunds independently lists five total seats for the 2020 RX 350 and provides rear-seat dimensions. [3] These support a two-row five-seat belt/anchor reference, but the sources opened here do not provide a verified 2020 manual diagram of each belt buckle, upper anchor, ISOFIX/LATCH point, or tether-anchor position. A production reference should therefore treat belt routing, buckle geometry, anchor covers and exact anchor locations as **not yet fully verified** for this task. Do not copy anchor locations from another model year, RX 350L, or hybrid without an exact-year check.

### Accessory and equipment configurations relevant to the cabin

The 2020 brochure identifies an available 8-inch or 12.3-inch touchscreen, available color Head-Up Display, new Android Auto, Apple CarPlay, Amazon Alexa compatibility, available heated/ventilated front seats, available heated second-row outboard seats, power-folding/reclining second-row seats, ambient LED illumination, and available 15-speaker Mark Levinson audio on applicable packages. [1] Edmunds lists 12.3-inch navigation/Mark Levinson, color HUD, heated/ventilated seating, and power-folding heated rear-seat options under 2020 RX 350 options. [3] CarPro’s test vehicle was a 2020 RX 350 F SPORT AWD with both F SPORT packages, 12.3-inch navigation, Mark Levinson audio, color HUD, heated/cooled seats, heated steering wheel, LED ambient illumination, and a panoramic sunroof/roof rails. [5]

These are configuration references, not a claim that every target vehicle has all of them. The F SPORT review’s as-tested vehicle had about $10,000 of options and therefore should not be used as a base-trim specification. [5]

## Coverage and remaining gaps

Covered with exact-year evidence: five-seat versus RXL distinction; AWD identity; steering-wheel control category; F SPORT wheel/spoke and meter differentiation; paddle-shifter statement for the reviewed F SPORT; digital tachometer/driver-information display observation; dashboard/instrument-cluster/steering-wheel/vent visual categories; front/rear climate-control references; touchscreen/HUD/audio/seat/ambient-light option families; source-specific rights restrictions.

Not fully verified: exact button legends and spoke geometry for a non-F SPORT RX 350 AWD; exact cluster face and warning-light arrangement for each trim; exact vent grille geometry; belt webbing routing, buckle shape, upper anchor height, ISOFIX/LATCH location and tether-anchor position; exact accessory/package combinations for a particular VIN or market; any dimensions. The next research pass should prioritize an accessible official 2020 RX owner-manual copy and a trim/package-specific factory equipment sheet. Video assets linked by Lexus were not reviewed as footage; do not claim their footage was inspected.
