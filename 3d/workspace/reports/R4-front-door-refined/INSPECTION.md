# Final image inspection — R4 refined

Inspected actual Blender outputs, 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-23. This is an agent assessment for owner review, not human acceptance. Rendered images are not manufacturing validation.

## Front view and three-quarter

Compared with the approved primary image and the saved original R4 view:

- The armrest now has a flatter top and front face. The rising grip joins the horizontal section more coherently, with a visible dark opening beneath it. Its reference-relative thickness, bend and lower-return contour remain approximate.
- The old folded speaker/pocket connection is removed from these views. The mouth is narrower and the large right-hand sloping structural panel now occupies the corresponding reference region. The opening is darker and less readable than the reference; the panel and wall transitions remain relatively plain.
- The release lever has a readable scoop and depth. The bezel still reads brighter and more graphic than the reference, and the switch/lever details remain simplified. The upper-right carrier spike is reduced, but the upper seal/roll end is still more straight-edged than the reference.
- Tan leather is less orange, walnut is darker and metal is less mirror-like. At whole-door scale the leather remains smoother and more uniform than the reference. Stitching is subtle and can disappear when the image is reduced.
- The speaker face shows a fine pattern, but horizontal banding is visible in the reduced overview. Its credibility must be judged in the final original close-up, not inferred from the shader's nominal hole pattern.

The primary image's projection and lighting are unknown. Matching aspect ratio and approximate framing aid comparison; they do not prove exact silhouette or depth alignment. The three-quarter view exposes inferred depth that the primary alone cannot establish.

## Close-ups and remaining views

The required final views were opened and assessed; observations follow.

### Handle and armrest close-up

The 1440 x 1000, 96-sample final image shows the new sloping deck, flatter front face, continuous bend into the grab and open gap beneath it. The seam at the bend and two stitch rows are visible at this scale. The upper grab attachment still meets the insert with a relatively abrupt crease, and the lower return remains a narrow, uniform ribbon compared with the reference's more integrated support. Leather grain is very fine and padding tension remains subdued; the broad top can still read as too smooth. The scooped release lever reads better than the old flat slab, but the bezel has angular/pointed lower corner transitions. These are unresolved visual details, not technical failures hidden by the data checks.

### Pocket and speaker close-up

The final 1440 x 1000 image shows a continuous shoulder-to-wall transition in place of the original folded overlap. The pocket has a visible back, floor and opening, separated from the sloping right structural section. The lip still has a sharp change near its leading turn, and the interior lacks the reference's more nuanced shaped/divided construction. The grille's staggered hole pattern is visible at close-up size, with an acoustic backing and satin surround; overview downsampling still produces banding. The speaker remains close to the lower outline. The courtesy lens and lower-panel surfaces are simplified. This view supports an improvement in the junction and grille, not an exact match to the approved image.

### Release housing and decorative trim

The dedicated final view confirms the lever's scooped upper surface, stepped root and recessed backing. Its shape is more readable than the old flat lever. The thin wood reveal follows the retained panel boundary. Walnut is substantially darker than R4, but still appears as a regular procedural grain rather than the primary image's richer irregular figuring. The satin bezel has fewer mirror-like highlights, yet its lower corners remain pointed/angular and the bottom strip is too emphatic. A small straight-edged upper-right seal termination remains visible. The existing simplified memory controls are retained; they were not embellished with invented labels.


### Neutral geometry and material presentation

Both final images were opened on 2026-11-23. The neutral view shows that the flatter armrest and revised pocket junction are geometric changes, independent of tan/black colour contrast. It also exposes the narrow lower grip return, angular release bezel, sharp insert leading tip and comparatively plain right-hand structural panel. The material presentation shows the pocket opening and revised lip from the opposite angle without the earlier folded shoulder. Its leading turn is still tight. Leather remains uniformly padded and the wood/controls are simplified. These images are retained without retouching.

### Assessment

This is a useful, review-ready improvement over R4, particularly the armrest crest, pocket layout/junction and lever depth. It does not fully match the primary reference's upholstery construction, nuanced surface transitions or material richness. The most important remaining differences are the grip's lower support/attachment, bezel corners, pocket sculpting and natural upholstery tension. Final visual acceptance belongs to the owner.
