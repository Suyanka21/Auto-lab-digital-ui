# Review and validation

A useful review compares the same camera/light conditions before and after, then exposes depth with an oblique view. Do not improve lighting between versions and attribute the entire improvement to geometry.

R4 used Blender 5.2.2 LTS, CPU Cycles, AgX Medium High Contrast, exposure 0, denoising, 64 samples for overviews/lab and 96 for close-ups. Front framing was 1440 x 804 to follow the primary's analytical 1200:670 frame. Other inspection views were 1440 x 1000. These are reproducible example settings, not required settings for every component.

Deliver front/reference comparison, three-quarter, close-ups of the current defects, neutral geometry and material presentation. The neutral override diagnoses construction; it cannot demonstrate leather/wood quality. Keep raw PNGs and source identifiers with any labelled/resized comparison sheets. No generative repainting of model evidence.

Record defects by region, visible symptom, likely geometry/material cause, chosen correction and remaining uncertainty. R4's intermediate previews caught a twisted moulding, floating stitches, an inflated armrest, a pinched speaker junction and an incorrectly oriented grip section. These were fixed in bounded iterations; remaining window-switch and minor trim details were accepted by the owner as benchmark limitations.

Run a fresh-open audit of the saved file, not just the in-memory scene. Check packed resources, named component collection, finite source/evaluated geometry, UVs, material assignments and evaluated counts. Add targeted construction probes where useful. An empty collection must fail. UV presence alone does not prove UV quality; no supplied script certifies global manifoldness or manufacturing fit.

Raster comparison metrics are advisory. R4's archived helper uses border segmentation and square resizing, confounded by backdrop, projection and lighting. Never turn IoU, histogram overlap or triangle count into a visual-fidelity percentage. Humans decide acceptance from actual images.

Record the owner's approval with the exact master hash and its scope. Preserve the earlier reports as dated evidence even if they say review was open. A later approval record supersedes that status, not the recorded defects or test limitations.
