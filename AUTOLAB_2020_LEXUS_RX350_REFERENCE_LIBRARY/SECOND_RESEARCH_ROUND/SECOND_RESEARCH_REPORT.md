# SECOND_RESEARCH_REPORT

**Target:** U.S.-market 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD, conventional two-row/five-seat interior  \n**Research date:** 2026-11-16  \n**Scope:** visual evidence, technical documentation, parts/catalogue evidence, videos, rights and unresolved reconstruction gaps.

## Executive assessment

This second round materially strengthens the evidence base for the major cabin layout and component vocabulary. The strongest conclusion is that the reconstruction target must remain the conventional-wheelbase, two-row, five-seat 2020 RX 350 AWD. RX 350L/RX L three-row evidence is not interchangeable. F SPORT evidence is useful for a related package, but its seats, steering wheel, meter, aluminium trim, pedals and scuff plates must not be generalized to a non-F SPORT RX 350 AWD.

The evidence is now sufficient for a defensible **categorical and positional** reconstruction of the front cockpit, centre console, five-seat rear bench, door-component families, floor/lower-cabin structure and major control groups. It is not sufficient for VIN-specific upholstery, exact stitch or perforation geometry, hidden fasteners, speaker layout, cabin dimensions, seat hardpoints, belt-anchor coordinates or a rights-cleared commercial image set.

No dimensions were inferred from photographs. Public access was never treated as commercial reuse permission. Video findings are limited to accessible transcripts, chapters and metadata; pages without inspectable footage are explicitly not treated as frame-reviewed evidence.

## What this round adds

The first round already contained official Lexus images, a brochure, specifications and general gaps. This round adds ten component-level research memos, exact-year gallery indexes, dealer and editorial feature descriptions, OEM parts-catalogue leads, a Lexus genuine-parts page with a 2020 left-rear trim-panel part number, an owner-manual operation trail, Toyota TIS access information, and timestamped rear-door removal evidence.

The most important new technical information is operational rather than dimensional. The owner-manual evidence supports 40/20/40 rear-seat folding/reclining, required headrest and centre-belt-buckle stowage before folding, rear belt side-pocket storage, release levers, LATCH anchors and top-tether brackets. OEM catalogues separate console body, shifter, courtesy lamp, dashboard, rear-seat, seat-track, floor-pan, rocker and door-trim assemblies. A 2020 rear-door repair video transcript identifies access plates, a window-switch screw, panel clips, wiring/cable disconnection, water-shield/latch access and T30 Torx/handle work. These findings describe assembly relationships, not universal fastener counts or dimensions.

## Identity and configuration rules

The brochure, Lexus pressroom and period specifications establish the 2020 RX family, available RX 350 AWD and the regular five-seat RX 350 versus RX 350L distinction. The standard cabin baseline is NuLuxe with power-adjustable front seats. Semi-aniline perforated leather, memory, heated/ventilated front seats, cushion extenders, wood/aluminium trim, HUD, Mark Levinson, moonroof/panorama roof and power rear-seat operation are package- or trim-dependent.

The 2020 model-year cockpit has an 8-inch touchscreen baseline and an available 12.3-inch pinch/zoom screen, with Lexus Remote Touchpad retained. The cabin also contains physical climate controls, a shifter, drive-mode selector, console storage, cupholders, a dedicated upright phone slot and USB connectivity. Cars.com’s period review distinguishes charge-only USB ports near the holder from a data-capable port in console storage for the reviewed configuration; this is not a universal build rule.

## Evidence by component

### Front seats

Evidence is strong for seat role, power-adjustment categories and optional material vocabulary. It is weaker for the actual target donor’s upholstery and close geometry. The research did not find a VIN-confirmed AWD photo set that simultaneously proves perforation, stitch pitch, bolster shape and power-control geometry. F SPORT evidence establishes enhanced bolsters and distinctive trim only for that package.

### Rear seats

The 40/20/40 reclining/folding bench, three adjustable headrests, fold-down armrest/cupholders, three-point belts and cargo-side release controls are well supported. Manual evidence gives the most useful operating constraints. It does not establish that folded seatbacks are perfectly flush with the cargo floor, and no dimensions were found.

### Dashboard and cockpit

The dashboard, steering, display, physical climate controls and Remote Touch relationship are well supported at layout level. Exact-year U.S. News and Edmunds galleries provide labelled categories for dashboard, instrument panel, steering wheel, cluster, audio, climate, vents, gear shift and door controls. Their labels are useful indexes, but extracted page text did not expose every image at pixel level.

### Centre console

The console architecture is well supported: touchscreen plus Remote Touchpad, selector, drive-mode control, cupholders, storage, armrest and phone slot. Exact cupholder count, storage volume, selector geometry, armrest dimensions and base-AWD close-up remain open.

### Door panels

OEM catalogues add substantial component vocabulary: front/rear trim panels, armrest covers, handles, pockets, window switches, bezels, memory/mirror modules, courtesy lamps, harnesses and premium-audio branches. A Lexus parts page identifies a 2020 left-rear trim-panel assembly, part 67640-06P30-C8. The visual gap remains a complete, inspectable four-door set from one exact AWD build.

### Roof and headliner

Factory sources support moonroof/panoramic-roof options, overhead interior/personal lights, sun visors and vanity mirrors. No exact headliner CAD, roof-opening dimensions, pillar-section drawings or clip coordinates were found. Starlight fibre-optic evidence is aftermarket/custom only, not factory 2020 RX evidence.

### Floor and lower cabin

The strongest fit-specific evidence is the AWD carpet-mat listing: four removable mats, nibbed backing and quarter-turn fasteners for the front mats, with a pedal-interference warning. Parts catalogues establish floor pans, rails, rocker, centre-pillar, sill and seat-track categories. No carpet dimensions, rail bolt spacing or bare-cabin teardown was found.

### Steering, controls and restraints

Steering-wheel audio/cruise/trip controls, tilt/telescopic adjustment and F SPORT paddle/wheel/meter distinctions are supported. Exact non-F SPORT button legends and spoke geometry remain unverified. Manual evidence supports general belt, headrest, LATCH and tether operation, but not anchor coordinates, buckle shape or webbing routing.

## Video findings

The strongest video evidence is transcript-supported rather than frame-extracted. The North Park Lexus walkaround maps door-side memory/window/mirror controls, power seats, display, Remote Touchpad, climate/seat controls, shifter, drive-mode selector, cupholders, sunroof and rear controls. The 2020 rear-door removal tutorial provides timestamps for trim access, screws, clips, wiring, water shield, latch and handle removal. The 2016–2022 A-pillar tutorial is useful as generation-range evidence only. Title-only or unavailable pages remain leads, not inspected footage.

## Rights and provenance

No collected item is classified as commercially permitted. Lexus pressroom assets carry explicit editorial-use limits. Dealer, editorial, OEM catalog, brochure mirror, forum and YouTube material remain controlled by their respective owners. The register records source URLs, evidence classes, rights status and limitations. The local source assets are research references and must not be shipped into the product or marketing material without separate permission or newly commissioned photography.

## Answer to the six requested questions

1. **How much stronger is the visual evidence?** It is substantially stronger for exact-year component categories and cabin relationships. It is not yet a complete multi-angle, VIN-confirmed visual dataset.
2. **Which components have sufficient evidence for accurate reconstruction?** The major two-row cabin layout, dashboard/console relationship, rear-seat architecture, major control families and broad floor/door assembly relationships are sufficiently evidenced for a categorical reconstruction.
3. **Which components still lack reliable evidence?** Exact seat upholstery geometry, door-card surface/stitching/speaker detail, roof/headliner construction, hidden restraints, dimensions and VIN-specific trim remain weak.
4. **What technical information was uncovered?** Rear-seat folding/stowage and restraint operation; OEM component naming and variant branches; 2020 rear-door removal sequence; floor/rail/rocker/seat-track categories; TIS as the legitimate path to controlled repair manuals and wiring diagrams.
5. **Single biggest obstacle?** No VIN-confirmed donor build with rights-cleared, multi-angle photography and legitimate technical documentation. Without that, exact grade/option geometry and material decisions remain uncertain.
6. **Have public avenues been exhausted?** The reasonable public avenues were searched broadly across official sources, dealers, editorial galleries, parts catalogues, forums and videos. Specific worthwhile next avenues remain: VIN/window sticker, owner-approved photography, legitimate TIS access, measured photography/photogrammetry and permission-cleared OEM diagrams.
