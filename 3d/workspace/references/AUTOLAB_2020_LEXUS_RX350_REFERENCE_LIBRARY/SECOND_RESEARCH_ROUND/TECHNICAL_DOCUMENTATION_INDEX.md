## Dimensions and measurements

No reliable interior dimensions, seat hardpoints, rail bolt spacing, trim-panel dimensions, anchor coordinates, roof-opening dimensions or speaker sizes were found. The brochure provides exterior dimensions, but they must not be used as interior measurements.

## Hidden construction

The public evidence establishes assembly categories and some removal relationships, but not complete hidden construction. The strongest leads are OEM catalogue branches and the timestamped 2020 rear-door removal transcript. Legitimate TIS access or owner-approved dismantling photography is still required for fasteners, clips, connectors, airbag routing and seat/door/headliner interfaces.

## Model-code and variant control

The sources repeatedly branch by RX 350 versus RX 350L, RX 450h, F SPORT, premium audio, HUD, power seats, colour, build origin, non-hybrid/hybrid and market. Use the five-seat RX 350 AWD as the baseline, but require a VIN/window sticker/build sheet before assigning optional geometry or material to a specific donor.
