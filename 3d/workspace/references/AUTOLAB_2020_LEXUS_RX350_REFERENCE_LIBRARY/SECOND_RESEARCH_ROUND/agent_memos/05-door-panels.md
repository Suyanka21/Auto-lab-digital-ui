# 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD (five-seat) interior door panels — second-round research memo

## Target and bottom line

This memo covers the North-American 2020 Lexus RX 350 five-passenger body, with emphasis on front/rear door cards, armrests, interior handles, switchgear, speakers, trim, and stitching. The 2020 RX 350 was offered with front-wheel drive or all-wheel drive, while the separate RX 350L was the three-row model. The official 2020 brochure explicitly distinguishes the five-passenger RX 350 from the RXL and states that RX 350 AWD was available [1]. A dealer model-feature page independently states five-passenger seating and identifies the base RX 350 interior as NuLuxe, with optional semi-aniline leather and wood/aluminum trim [5].

The best exact-year visual indexes are the U.S. News 2020 interior gallery (17 labeled interior images, including “Door Controls”) [2] and Edmunds’ 2020 RX 350 gallery (18 interior images, including driver-window controls and interior detail) [6]. The text extraction confirms the image labels and year/model, but it does not expose every underlying image at full resolution. Therefore, these pages establish the existence and labeling of exact-year imagery, not a complete close visual inspection of every door-card feature. Lexus’s official 2020 brochure provides contextual interior photography and equipment/trim statements, but the brochure images are marketing views rather than dedicated front- and rear-door-card close-ups [1].

The strongest technical parts evidence is the Lexus-branded parts page for a 2020 left-rear door trim panel assembly, part 67640-06P30-C8, and the 2020 RX 350 front-door interior-trim catalog. These establish that the door-card assembly is model/year-specific and enumerate variant-dependent parts such as door trim panels, inside handles and covers, pockets, armrest covers, window switches, switch bezels, memory/mirror-control modules, courtesy lamps, harnesses, and premium-audio/seat-material combinations [3] [4]. They do not provide a complete exploded illustration in the extracted text, nor do they prove which exact grade, color, audio package, or build plant applies to a particular photographed vehicle.

- **Front door cards:** Exact-year support from the 2020 front-door OEM catalog, with multiple material/audio/color variants [4]. Exact-year editorial image indexes identify driver-window controls and interior detail [2] [6]. A dedicated, fully inspectable front-door-card close-up was not established in fetched text.
- **Rear door cards:** Exact-year OEM support through the official Lexus left-rear trim-panel assembly and part number 67640-06P30-C8 [3]. The official brochure and galleries show rear-cabin context, but a dedicated rear-door-card close-up was not established.

- **Handles:** Catalog explicitly lists inside handles and handle covers, left and right [4]. The fetched pages do not establish detailed handle geometry or finish.
- **Switches/modules:** Catalog explicitly lists window switches, switch bezels, memory switch, mirror-control modules, courtesy lamps and harnesses [4]. Gallery captions support an exact-year driver-window-control image index [2] [6].

A fetched source did not provide a dedicated, inspectable door-speaker close-up or speaker part/diagram for the target build.
- **Trim/materials:** Official brochure and dealer page establish NuLuxe, leather/semi-aniline, wood/aluminum, and F SPORT scored-aluminum options [1] [5]. Catalog confirms multiple color/material/audio combinations [4].
- **Stitching:** Stitching is suggested at marketing-view upholstery scale in brochure/gallery imagery but is not technically or close-visually verified in the fetched text. No stitch count, thread color, seam spacing, or dimension should be inferred.

## Gaps and handling guidance

The key remaining gap is a fully inspectable set of dedicated front-left, front-right, rear-left and rear-right door-card photographs tied to a 2020 RX 350 AWD non-F SPORT grade. Current sources provide exact-year gallery indexes and OEM catalog facts, but not a verified image set for every door position. There is also no fetched door-speaker diagram or close-up tied to one specific audio package, and no reliable close visual evidence for stitching or surface grain.

For an AutoLab reference package, use [1] for year/body/trim context, [3] and [4] for model-specific component and variant logic, and [2]/[6] as pointers to exact-year editorial imagery. Keep F SPORT images separated from regular RX 350 images. Do not mix RX 350L three-row imagery into the five-seat target. Do not infer dimensions, material codes, speaker sizes, stitch spacing, or AWD-specific door-card geometry from these sources. Any production use of imagery must be separately cleared with the rights holder.
