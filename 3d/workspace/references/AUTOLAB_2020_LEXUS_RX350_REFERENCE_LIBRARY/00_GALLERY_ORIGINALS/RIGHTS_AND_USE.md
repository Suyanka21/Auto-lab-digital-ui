## Branding boundary

The 2020 Lexus RX 350 is AutoLab's vehicle-design reference. The showroom is an AutoLab product, not a Lexus product. Logos, badges, wordmarks, the steering-wheel centre emblem, branded interface screens and marketing artwork visible in these photographs must not be reproduced in AutoLab models, textures or interface. The steering-wheel centre in the model stays neutral and unbranded.
