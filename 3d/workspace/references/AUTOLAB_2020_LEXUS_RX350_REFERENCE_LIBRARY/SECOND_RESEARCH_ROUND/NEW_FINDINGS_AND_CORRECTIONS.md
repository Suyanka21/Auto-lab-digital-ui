# NEW_FINDINGS_AND_CORRECTIONS

## Confirmed or strengthened

- The base target is the conventional two-row/five-seat RX 350 AWD, not RX 350L. This is now supported by the brochure, exact-year databases, dealer/specification pages and video narration.
- The rear bench is 40/20/40 split, reclining/folding, with manual or package-dependent power operation. Headrest and centre-belt-buckle stowage before folding is an operating constraint, not a visual guess.
- The 2020 cockpit’s major architecture is a touchscreen plus retained Remote Touchpad, physical climate controls, shifter, drive-mode selector, storage, cupholders and phone slot.
- Door cards are materially and electrically variant-dependent. Parts catalogues show different panels for NuLuxe/leather/semi-aniline, premium audio, F SPORT and colour combinations.
- Factory starlight lighting was not established. Starlight references are aftermarket/custom and must remain separate from factory geometry.
- The current AutoLab roadmap’s warning about future multi-part loading and shared material instances is reinforced: the evidence package covers front cabin, doors, rear seats and headliner as distinct zones, but no public source justifies silently merging them into one material or one GLB.

## Corrections to earlier assumptions

- Do not describe any gallery image as a verified 2020 RX 350 AWD build unless its caption or vehicle record says so. Exact-year/model evidence is not exact drivetrain/trim evidence.
- Do not generalize F SPORT enhanced bolsters, steering wheel, meter, aluminium trim, pedals or scuff plates to the base RX 350 AWD.
- Do not use brochure or dealer option descriptions as proof that a photographed vehicle has the option.
- Do not claim that folded rear seats are flush with the cargo floor; that was not established.
- Do not claim a complete visual review of videos whose pages exposed only title, metadata, transcript or chapter text.
- Do not derive dimensions from photos, exterior specification tables or perspective relationships.
- Do not treat public URLs, official hosting or downloaded files as commercial reuse permission.

## Reconciliation with existing AutoLab contracts

The new research supports preserving the existing named seat slots, ORM-alpha thread-mask contract, trim UV scale, local decoders, camera limits and current triangle/draw-call/GLB budgets. It also supports keeping geometry evidence separate from material evidence: seat silhouette and seam placement require different evidence from leather roughness, perforation scale or stitch colour. The new research does not justify changing any viewer code, asset, material name, camera preset or performance budget.
