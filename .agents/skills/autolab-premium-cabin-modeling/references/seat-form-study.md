# Seat form before upholstery: lessons from R5-A

The seat study transfers R4's section-controlled padding, not its door dimensions. Its visual acceptance remains an owner decision. See the [source, cameras, measured checks and evidence](../../../../3d/R5-A/README.md).

- **Change section direction at the right problem.** For the backrest, control half-width, lumbar contact depth, rear thickness and forward wing reach *independently at each height*. For the cushion, run a closed side-view section from the centre dish over the front roll and back underneath, then sweep it across the width. A longitudinal loft capped at the most visible point produced a pinched nose. The continuous sagittal section removed that crease without adding upholstery seams.
- **Shape the foam before the cover.** Sample the actual smoothed foam with rays at the intended rear carrier footprint; offset the exposed skin and give it an inside return that seats into the foam. A separate guessed silhouette floated behind the shoulder. Offsetting normals on a tightly curved open rim introduced spikes. Paired front/back samples connected by quad walls were easier to inspect from the side. For an external cover, test a small positive exposed offset across the field, and inspect the return separately; neither proves global fit.
- **Keep handed parts out of the shared upholstery cage.** Use the same section family for the lower apron and side shields. Give the outboard controls separate solids; check their backs actually enter the shield. Keep posts and sleeves separate; test that posts meet both foam forms. Mirroring a completed seat reverses more than the controls: resolve side-specific hardware independently when that variant is authorised.
- **Distinguish placement from scale.** Record the imported coordinate conversion, retain station centre and seat-pan/head landmarks, then change only the driver assembly in a diagnostic copy of the cabin. Use identical before/after cameras and unchanged context meshes. A surface-intersection query is useful, but a missing floor, roof, door or occupant is not evidence of clearance.
- **Compare like with like.** The driver's outboard side in runtime coordinates may face the other way from an isolated reference. Keep the native render. If reflecting an image to compare its silhouette, label the reflection and do not present it as a handedness or fit test. Use orthographic front, side and rear and a neutral material override: changing camera angle or hide colour can conceal a section error.

These techniques establish editable volumes and colour regions only. They do not prescribe perforation, quilting, hidden safety mechanisms, runtime topology or final geometry.
