## Static assembly measurements

Both seats span Z = 244.00 to 1304.94 mm. Their inboard bounding planes are 308.06 mm apart before accounting for the console. All 45 evaluated components per seat, including the thread curves, were checked against the production dashboard and five console reference meshes. No triangle-surface intersections were found. The seat envelopes also have positive lateral separation from each other.

The nearest sampled seat-vertex distances to the console and dashboard were **34.12 mm** and **135.65 mm** respectively on each side. These describe one parked pose. Door clearances, occupant fit and the swept volume through seat travel remain unverified. **Both sets of rail feet are 66 mm below the production seat minimum.** A floor and mounting-point reference is needed to resolve anchorage.

The fresh-open comparison checked the driver against the approved source, the passenger's corresponding vertices, reversed face loops, face normals, all UV layers, punch margins, shape-key endpoints and stitch paths. All six perforation inputs and both lumbar controls operated independently. The master data audit found 76 mesh components and 333,384 evaluated mesh triangles excluding curve thread and the placement context. The assembly is a detailed editable master.
