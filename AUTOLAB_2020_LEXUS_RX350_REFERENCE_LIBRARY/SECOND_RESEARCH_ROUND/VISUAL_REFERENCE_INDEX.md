# VISUAL_REFERENCE_INDEX

This index consolidates the visual evidence found in the second round. Exact-year does not automatically mean exact AWD grade or exact option package. The evidence class is preserved for every source.

## Front seats

- **Primary exact-year visual leads:** U.S. News 2020 RX 350 interior gallery, especially the labelled Front Seat and Rear Seat categories [4]; Edmunds’ 2020 gallery, which separates regular RX 350 and F SPORT captions [5]. These are useful indexes for locating seat silhouettes, front-cabin framing and related controls, but the fetched text did not expose all image pixels.
- **Configuration evidence:** The 2020 brochure and Lexus dealer pages support NuLuxe baseline, power-adjustment categories, optional semi-aniline perforated leather, memory, heated/ventilated seats and cushion extenders [2]. Treat as offered equipment, not a particular donor build.
- **F SPORT support:** F SPORT sources show enhanced bolsters, contrast stitching and distinctive trim. Keep these images in a separate F SPORT evidence bucket.

## Rear seats

- **Exact-year/model evidence:** U.S. News and Edmunds gallery categories include Rear Seat [4] [5]. The pages do not certify AWD or package per image.
- **Geometry/operation evidence:** The owner manual supports 40/20/40 folding/reclining, headrest and centre-belt-buckle stowage, belt side pockets and release operations [3]. This is technical evidence, not a close visual reference.
- **Supporting visual context:** Dealer walkaround and review material identify rear armrest/cupholders, sliding/reclining behaviour and rear A/C relationships, but the exact photographed build is not always established.

## Dashboard and cockpit

- **Official exact-year:** Lexus pressroom assets and the local copies in `source_assets/official_media/` show official 2020 RX cabin context [1]. The pressroom rights notice makes these reference-only.
- **Gallery index:** U.S. News and Edmunds label dashboard, instrument panel, steering wheel, audio system, climate controls, air vents, gear shift and door controls [4] [5].
- **F SPORT comparison:** CarPro and Lexus F SPORT materials support the unique F SPORT wheel, meter, paddle, aluminium trim, pedals and scuff-plate differences [12].

## Centre console

- **Exact-year relationship:** Official media, brochure and period reviews support touchscreen plus Remote Touchpad, selector, drive-mode control, cupholders, storage and upright phone slot [1] [2] [11].
- **Useful close-detail lead:** Cars.com records the phone slot and distinguishes nearby charge-only USB from data-capable console storage USB in the reviewed configuration [11]. This is not a universal equipment guarantee.

## Door panels

- **Exact-year indexes:** U.S. News’ Door Controls category and Edmunds’ Driver’s Window Controls/Interior Detail categories are the most useful public visual leads [4] [5].
- **Assembly evidence:** Lexus parts sources identify trim panels, handles, armrest covers, pockets, switches, bezels, memory/mirror modules, courtesy lamps and harnesses; the Lexus Parts Center identifies a 2020 left-rear trim-panel assembly, part 67640-06P30-C8 [6] [7].
- **Video:** The rear-door repair tutorial provides transcript-supported access/removal sequence [8], but it is not a complete visual atlas.

## Roof and headliner

- **Factory:** Brochure and owner-manual evidence support option-dependent moonroof/panorama roof, overhead lighting, visors and vanity mirrors [2] [3].
- **Custom:** Starlight headliner material is aftermarket/custom evidence only. It must not be treated as a 2020 Lexus factory reference.

## Floor and lower cabin

- **Factory/accessory:** AWD carpet-mat listing supports removable four-piece mats, nibbed backing and front quarter-turn fasteners. OEM catalogues support floor pan, rails, rockers, sills, seat-track and console categories.
- **Dismantling:** A fourth-generation owner thread provides a centre-console trim sequence, but it is not a VIN-specific service procedure.

## Steering, restraints and controls

- Brochure, editorial reviews and manual evidence support steering-wheel control families, F SPORT distinctions, general restraint operation, LATCH/top-tether scope and climate/vent relationships [2] [3] [12]. Exact button geometry and anchor coordinates remain open.

## Rights interpretation

Every image and video remains controlled by the original publisher, manufacturer, photographer, dealer, forum contributor or creator. No item is cleared for commercial reuse. The local files are internal research references only.
