# R6-B rear-bench inspection

**For owner visual review; not production approval.** Start with `sheets/primary.jpg`. The source image and Cycles render are not the same camera or lighting setup. Use the native close-ups, not the resized sheet, to judge grain, stitching and punch density.

| In the Cycles views | Difference from the rear-bench primary |
| --- | --- |
| `three-quarter`, `front`: the yoke sits above three vertical lumbar pads on each side. | The pads stop higher above the cushion, leaving a wider plain lower band. The diagonal shoulder-to-bolster seam climbs less steeply toward the outer edge. These are the most visible differences at bench scale. |
| `backrest`: the insert padding turns down into separate sewn edges; the punch pattern stops short of each edge. | The dark punches are more regular and conspicuous than the primary's fine pattern. The lower pad ends cast a stronger shadow. The rear uses the approved front-seat finish, not an independently matched physical hide. |
| `armrest`, `front`: the plain centre leaf has padded edges, a short pull and a tonal stitch course. | The straight dark gaps at the sides frame the armrest more strongly than in the primary. The centre cushion has two extra lengthwise seams; it reads as a separate seating position rather than an unbroken strip. |
| `three-quarter`, `cabin`: the cushion fronts are lower and the outer inserts wrap over the crest. | The cushion faces still look more uniform across the three positions, and the exposed foot plates simpler than in the primary. No floor geometry was inferred to hide them. |

**Construction and scale.** The 44 editable cover pieces close into five sewn covers, with 29 separate thread curves. Each side has independently switchable shoulder, lumbar and cushion perforation. All three buckle release faces clear the new panels. At the same lateral centre and Y = 792 mm, the front crest is 7.22 mm lower on each cushion than R6-A. No bench bounding plane shifted more than 0.84 mm; the headrest and foot heights are unchanged. These measurements are of the Blender model, not of a vehicle.

**Assembly limits.** The rear and front row envelopes clear each other by 246 mm along the cabin's fore–aft axis in the static pose. This is not occupant legroom. The approximately 66 mm front-seat mounting discrepancy and rear-bench floor anchorage remain open. Armrest operation, seat travel, restraints, door clearance and installed fit have not been validated. No runtime conversion, floor reconstruction or production change is part of this checkpoint.
