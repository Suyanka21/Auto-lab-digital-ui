# 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD (five-seat) — roof, headliner, overhead-console and upper-cabin reference memo

## Bottom line

The factory 2020 Lexus RX 350 is a five-seat, fourth-generation RX cabin. The standard upper-cabin reference is a conventional headliner with front overhead interior/personal lights, sun visors and vanity mirrors, rear assist grips, and A/B/C/D-pillar trim; a moonroof or panorama glass roof is an option/package item rather than evidence that every RX 350 has a panoramic roof. The 2020 U.S. brochure and dealer specifications consistently identify the optional feature as a panorama/panoramic glass roof or power tilt/slide moonroof, while the owner manual treats the moon roof as “if equipped.” [1] [2] [3] [4]

No inspected factory source supports a “starlight” fiber-optic ceiling as original equipment on the 2020 RX. The starlight sources reviewed here are explicitly aftermarket kit/install material. One YouTube page is inspectable as a transcript and plainly describes drilling the removed headliner, threading fiber-optic lights, adding a power box, and replacing an earlier kit; it must not be used as a factory-cabin reference. [8]

## Vehicle identity and scope

The target is the U.S.-market 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD, five-passenger body (not RX 350L three-row, RX 450h, or RX 450hL). The official 2020 brochure identifies RX 350 as the five-passenger RX and lists front-wheel drive or available all-wheel drive. Edmunds separately lists the 2020 RX 350 AWD trim and total seating of five. [1] [3]

Trim/package matters. Lexus and dealer sources describe the panorama glass roof as an available/optional interior feature. The Edmunds configuration page describes a “Power Tilt/Slide Moonroof w/Aluminum Roof Rails” as a priced exterior option. This does not establish that a specific photographed vehicle has the option; it only establishes factory availability for the model/configuration. [2] [3] [4]

## Component findings

### Moonroof / panoramic roof

The strongest factory evidence is the 2020 Lexus brochure. Its equipment/feature material uses “Panorama glass roof” in the 2020 RX feature set, and the dealer feature page lists “Panoramic glass roof” as an individual upgrade option for the RX 350. Edmunds’ model-year configuration page lists “Power Tilt/Slide Moonroof w/Aluminum Roof Rails” as a priced option. These sources establish factory availability and terminology, not dimensions, exact aperture geometry, or standard-vs-option status for an individual VIN. [1] [2] [3]

For reference construction, treat the roof as an option-dependent opening/roof module: a standard-roof RX and a moonroof/panoramic-roof RX should not be merged into one universal headliner pattern. The owner manual’s table of contents and pictorial index label “Moon roof” with an “if equipped” marker and direct the reader to page 100. [4] The manual therefore supports the existence of a factory moonroof control/operation section, but it does not by itself prove that every 2020 RX 350 AWD has the roof.

### Overhead console / front roof controls

The owner manual’s interior pictorial index identifies “Personal lights” and “Interior light” in the front roof area, alongside the inside rear-view mirror and sun visors. Its table of contents places the interior-light material at pages 416–417. The inspected manual also contains the moon-roof section at page 100. This is the appropriate factory technical reference for the presence and terminology of the overhead lighting/control area, but the text extraction does not provide a dimensioned drawing or a complete trim-level parts breakdown. [4]

A secondary 2016–2022 RX repair video page found during search is not used as primary evidence because its page was not independently inspected beyond its title/snippet. The factory owner manual and the official 2020 Lexus pressroom image library are safer provenance anchors for production reference work. [4] [5]

### Sun visors and vanity mirrors

The owner manual’s table of contents places “Sun visors” at page 428 and “Vanity mirrors” at page 429. The pictorial index also places the sun visors in the front roof/headliner zone. This technically establishes the factory components and their manual terminology for the relevant RX generation. It does not establish color, exact shape in every trim, mirror-cover construction, or whether a specific roof option changes visor geometry. [4]

### Grab handles / assist grips

The owner manual’s interior pictorial index and contents establish the upper-cabin interior as a documented system, but the searchable extraction did not expose a dedicated “assist grip/grab handle” heading with a complete diagram in the returned excerpt. A ClubLexus thread specifically about the 2016–2022 RX asks whether an A-pillar grab handle can be added; the opening post says the RX does not have the GX/LX-style A-pillar grab handle, and a reply warns about the side airbag. This is useful generation-specific field evidence: do not automatically add a front A-pillar grab handle to a 2020 RX reference. [6]

The same thread is not a factory parts catalog and is not sufficient to define every rear assist-grip location, color, fastener, or trim-specific variation. Use it as Class C contextual evidence only. Do not infer dimensions or mounting coordinates from it.

### Pillars

The inspected owner manual contains SRS curtain-airbag and interior pictorial-index references, and the ClubLexus discussion makes the A-pillar/side-airbag conflict explicit. For a visual/reference package, preserve the factory relationship between the upper trim and curtain-airbag zone: A-pillar, B-pillar and rear/C-pillar trim are safety-adjacent interior panels, not interchangeable decorative strips. The evidence gathered here establishes the safety and generation context, but does not provide dimensioned pillar-section drawings or a verified complete 2020 trim-color map. [4] [6]

### Headliner

Factory sources support a conventional upholstered headliner/roof trim system with integrated front lighting, visor attachments, roof controls where equipped, and upper trim interfaces. The 2020 brochure and official pressroom page provide model-year identity and factory imagery/press assets; the owner manual provides the technical operating context for the moonroof and interior-light systems. [1] [4] [5]

The inspected sources do not establish headliner thickness, substrate, adhesive, seam layout, attachment clips, roof-opening dimensions, or exact clearance to curtain airbags. No dimensions should be added to the AutoLab package from this memo.

## Factory versus aftermarket starlight distinction

The Dynamic Appearance product page is a retail listing for a “Customs Unlimited Ultimate Starlight Headliner Kit.” Its page displays a kit/product image gallery and describes a kit rather than Lexus original equipment. This is Class D aftermarket provenance. It can establish that retrofit starlight kits exist and that the visual effect should not be attributed to the 2020 RX factory cabin. Public retail accessibility is not commercial-use permission for the product photography. [7]

The inspected YouTube page is titled “WE ADDED MORE LIGHTS TO THE LEXUS STAR HEADLINER!” Its transcript describes a Lexus being disassembled: sun visors, overhead light/infotainment console, hand holders/assist grips, pillar trim and the headliner are removed; the creator drills/threads roughly 430 fiber-optic lights, uses zip ties/hot glue and a power box, and replaces an earlier setup. This establishes an aftermarket installation process and the physical interfaces a retrofit may disturb. It does not establish a 2020 model year, AWD, five-seat configuration, factory roof design, or factory provenance. The page is inspectable as text/transcript, but no independent frame-by-frame visual review is claimed here. [8]

Accordingly, any “stars,” fiber-optic points, drilled holes, exposed wiring, blackened headliner, or non-OEM light engine visible in a candidate image should be labeled aftermarket/custom unless a separate vehicle-specific provenance record proves otherwise. The factory 2020 RX references in this memo contain no evidence that Lexus delivered a starlight headliner on this model year.

## Evidence-class key

- **Class A — OEM/official:** Lexus 2020 brochure, Lexus pressroom, and Toyota/Lexus owner-manual PDF.
- **Class B — reputable vehicle data/photo publisher:** Edmunds model-year specs and photo-gallery pages.
- **Class C — dealer or enthusiast contextual evidence:** Lexus dealer feature pages and ClubLexus forum discussion.
- **Class D — aftermarket/custom:** retrofit product listing and custom-install video.

## Gaps and recommended next verification

- No source inspected here supplies verified 2020 RX 350 AWD headliner CAD, roof-opening dimensions, pillar-section drawings, clip locations, assist-grip coordinates, or trim-color codes.
- The owner-manual PDF should be paired with a vehicle-specific 2020 RX repair manual/parts catalog if exact service geometry is needed; do not infer dimensions from the manual pictorial index.
- A specific vehicle’s roof option should be verified from its window sticker/build sheet or VIN-linked equipment record before selecting a panoramic-roof reference.
- Official Lexus pressroom images are useful for provenance, but each candidate image must be inspected individually for visibility of the upper cabin and cleared under the page’s editorial-only rights terms.
- Any starlight treatment must remain labeled custom/aftermarket unless a separate, vehicle-specific OEM record proves otherwise.
