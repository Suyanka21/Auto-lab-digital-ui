# Reference Gaps

The following gaps materially limit exact AutoLab geometry decisions:

1. **Owner/VIN-specific configuration.** The package does not establish the target vehicle’s exact grade, seat covering, trim, screen size, moonroof/headliner configuration or option package.
2. **Exact five-seat rear cabin.** The rear-seat and rear-door reference set is incomplete for a U.S. 2020 RX 350 AWD. RX 350L and RX 450hL imagery must not be substituted without marking the mismatch.
3. **Door-card construction.** Public parts pages provide leads but not a rights-cleared, measured exploded assembly for the exact target grade.
4. **Seat underside and adjustment hardware.** Close-up references for track covers, switches, gap lines, foam transitions and underside geometry are incomplete.
5. **Headliner and roof.** Exact roof console, handles, sunshade, moonroof surround and headliner seam references are missing.
6. **Material calibration.** No package item establishes calibrated Lexus colour, roughness or grain values. Physical samples or controlled photography would be needed for production-quality material matching.
7. **Dimensions.** No verified component dimensions were extracted from the public photographs. Do not use image scaling as measurement.
8. **Rights-cleared product imagery.** No image is classified commercial-use permitted. Obtain Lexus permission, commission original photography or create original reference photography before shipping any visual into the product.
9. **Service documentation.** Toyota/Lexus service manuals and detailed exploded diagrams are generally access-controlled or market/VIN-specific; no paywall or access control was bypassed.

## Most valuable next references

- Owner-provided photographs of the actual 2020 RX 350 AWD interior from fixed, repeatable camera positions.
- VIN/grade confirmation and the exact window sticker or build sheet.
- Legitimately obtained OEM parts diagrams for front seat, dashboard, centre console, front/rear door trim and headliner.
- Measured dimensions or photogrammetry captured with permission.
- Physical material swatches photographed under a calibrated or documented lighting setup.
- An owner-approved close-up set of stitching, perforation, seat controls, trim joints and door-panel transitions.
