# Runtime delivery: a separate authorised task

Retain the detailed Blender master and derive a candidate outside `public/`. The current AutoLab budget applies to the complete cabin per treatment: **80,000 decoded triangles, 24 primitives (draw-call proxy), 5,242,880 bytes**. R4 refined alone has 719,424 evaluated master triangles. It is not a browser asset and was not validated as one.

1. Allocate the full-cabin budget before reducing an individual component. Measure contributions by component and material; reserve room for remaining parts. R3's 79,004 triangles left only 996, proving why local component passes did not establish a sustainable complete-cabin result.
2. Retopologise by silhouette, deformation/curvature and visible junctions. Reduce invisible subdivisions, not meaningful openings. Bake micrograin, perforations and suitable seam detail; preserve any geometry required by treatments that lack a validated mask alternative.
3. Bake supported base-colour/normal/roughness/metallic/AO maps. Procedural noise, complex alpha and Blender shading are not automatically translated to glTF. Check UV0, tangent handedness and alpha semantics after packing and compression.
4. Preserve existing material names and actual assignments. The seven bindings are `MAT_Seat_Primary`, `MAT_Seat_Secondary`, `MAT_Seat_Stitching`, `MAT_Steering_Leather`, `MAT_Dashboard_Trim`, `MAT_Cockpit_Accent`, `MAT_Cockpit_Black`. The actual baseline also has console slots; consult its manifest, not a seven-material assumption. `MAT_Console_Carbon` is the shared decorative finish for dashboard/console and candidate door trim, even when the preview is wood.
5. Preserve Classic, Diamond and Perforated; primary/secondary and single/two-tone semantics; actual stitching and Diamond mask behaviour; steering/dashboard overrides; camera coordinates/orbit limits; Retry and KTX2/Draco lifecycle. A door's master-material names are not permission to add live slots. Independent door colours, headliner and starlight controls require an explicit interface decision.
6. Use the repository's candidate asset gate against decoded geometry and compressed final bytes. KTX2/UASTC textures and Draco are existing contracts. Run Khronos validation and baseline comparisons. Report individual failures rather than hiding them behind a summary PASS.
7. In an authorised integration task, verify the real viewer: treatment switching, two-tone and single colour, thread recolouring on both seats, shared trim, four cameras, loading/error/retry, texture decode, desktop and actual phone behaviour. Measure frame/render statistics; distinguish draw calls from primitive proxies and observed measurements from estimates.

Relevant AutoLab sources are `src/viewer/configuration-adapter.ts`, `src/viewer/RuntimeViewer.tsx`, `src/config/lexus-camera-presets.ts`, `src/config/lexus-runtime-assets.ts`, `src/lib/three/thread-mask.ts`, `src/lib/three/ktx2-runtime.ts`, `scripts/validate-glb.mjs` and the scene manifests. Re-read current code before a future integration; a handover snapshot can become stale.

No automatic live-GLB replacement, budget increase or deployment follows a successful master or candidate gate.
