Collection date: 2026-11-16
Target: 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD, five-seat fourth-generation RX interior.
Purpose: traceable internal visual and technical reference package for future AutoLab engineering work.

## Important rights rule

This package is a research library, not a commercial asset pack. The Lexus pressroom page explicitly limits its media to editorial use and prohibits advertising/marketing use without appropriate permission. Dealer, marketplace, stock and YouTube-derived items are classified as reference-only or unverified unless a licence is explicitly established. Public accessibility does not imply commercial reuse.

## Model-year and market cautions

The target is the U.S.-style 2020 RX 350 AWD. The European newsroom page describes the 2020 RX family and may differ in equipment, trim names and market packaging. RX 350L, RX 450h and F SPORT references must not be silently merged into the target base configuration. Exact grade, seat upholstery, trim and screen size should be treated as configuration variables until confirmed by the owner’s vehicle or VIN.
