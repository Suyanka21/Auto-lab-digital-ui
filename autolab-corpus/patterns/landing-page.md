# Landing Page Pattern

## Objective

Create a premium editorial introduction that leads naturally into the Studio.

## Structure

1. Compact navigation.
2. Cinematic automotive-interior hero.
3. Primary headline: **Make it personal.**
4. Supporting copy about bespoke interior materials, colours, and finishes.
5. Primary CTA: **Enter the Studio**.
6. Editorial sections for Material, Colour, Cabin Trim, and Ambient Illumination.
7. Brief explanation of the configuration experience.
8. Clear route into the Studio.

## Visual treatment

- Warm ivory base.
- Large, calm automotive imagery.
- Editorial headline hierarchy.
- Restrained cyan accents.
- Fine borders and subtle surface separation.
- No generic SaaS feature grid.

## Interaction

- CTA leads to `/studio`.
- Section entrances may use subtle opacity and translate transitions.
- Do not obstruct reading with parallax or heavy animation.

## Content constraints

- No testimonials.
- No fabricated statistics.
- No pricing.
- No availability claims.
- No invented awards or partnerships.
