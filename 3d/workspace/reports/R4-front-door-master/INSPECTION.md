# R4 render inspection record

Actual final images were opened for inspection; this record distinguishes visible observations from data validation.

## Front-reference and three-quarter

- Primary-reference handedness and component arrangement are recognisable.
- Three-quarter view exposes an open pocket and space beneath the grip; those are modelled depths.
- Leather remains too uniformly smooth and orange under this lighting; the reference has darker, more broken response.
- Broad palmrest crest and a thin underside loop differ from the reference's upholstered support construction.
- Release lever is visibly simplified; bezel bends are abrupt.
- Upper trim end is sharp and protruding. Insert leading corner also reads pointed.
- Speaker face reads too solid at this scale, and the ring sits very close to the lower outline.
- These observations do not amount to visual approval.

## Final handle / release close-up

- Full-size 96-sample image shows the projected double stitch rows on the insert and curved grip/palmrest. They are no longer invisible as in low-resolution previews.
- Fine grain is visible on the black roll and tan insert. This improves surface readability, but does not add the reference's natural creasing or padding tension.
- The release lever remains a flat-looking silhouette. Its bezel has conspicuous pinched/abrupt bends.
- The underside return is too thin and loop-like; armrest thickness distribution remains too uniform.
- A small dark mark on the grip and sharp leading insert tip remain unresolved visual defects.

## Final pocket / speaker close-up

- Pocket opening and side-wall depth are visible; the front wall no longer crosses the speaker face.
- Fine perforation pattern is visible at full size, although the overview reads comparatively solid.
- A pinched/folded transition remains where the speaker's upper-right shoulder meets the pocket lip. This is a geometry/junction defect, not a material success.
- Pocket lip is too continuous and straight compared with the reference's shaped opening and divided interior.
- Speaker ring is too close to the bottom silhouette. Courtesy lamp remains a simplified lens without reference-level detail.

## Final neutral geometry view

The clay override makes the speaker-to-pocket pinch, pointed insert corner, sharp top trim termination and thin under-grip return visible without the coloured materials. These are shape/junction issues. The pocket cavity remains visible in neutral shading. This image is diagnostic and is not evidence of material quality.

## Final material presentation

The opposite three-quarter angle retains the same studio lighting and materials. It exposes the speaker-to-pocket fold more strongly than the frontal view, confirming that this is not merely a frontal shading artefact. The upper trim end remains sharp, release shape flat, and tan upholstery overly uniform. This view is retained without retouching or geometry changes. The master is suitable for a candid review checkpoint, not a recommendation for visual acceptance.

## Final material lab

Left to right: tan leather, black leather, walnut, perforated speaker material. Actual 64-sample render shows grain and roughness breakup on both leather samples, directional wood and the fine grille pattern. The wood still reads as a regular procedural approximation, and the leather samples do not demonstrate natural wrinkles or upholstery tension. Lab response is retained as evidence, not a visual-quality PASS.
