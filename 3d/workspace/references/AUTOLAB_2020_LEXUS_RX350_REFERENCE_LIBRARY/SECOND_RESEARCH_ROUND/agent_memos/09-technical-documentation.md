# 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD (five-seat) interior — technical-documentation research memo

**Research date:** 2026-11-16  
**Target:** U.S.-market 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD, standard two-row/five-seat RX body (not RX 350L three-row; not RX 450h; not a trim-specific F SPORT interior unless explicitly called out).  
**Scope:** Official Lexus/Toyota manuals and technical-access pages, OEM parts catalogs/exploded-category pages, service/removal availability, dimensions, and model/trim applicability.  
**Evidence key used here:** **A** = manufacturer primary source or manufacturer-hosted manual/specification; **B** = OEM/Genuine Lexus parts catalog or authorized dealer catalog; **C** = secondary reproduction/host of a manufacturer brochure; **D** = user-generated or non-verifiable material. Evidence class is about evidentiary provenance, not permission to reuse.

## Findings that are safe to carry into a reference package

The 2020 U.S. RX family is directly documented by the Lexus pressroom as a 2020 model, and the pressroom links a manufacturer product-specification PDF specifically titled “2020 Lexus RX RX 350 Specifications.” The pressroom also distinguishes the standard RX 350 from the RX 350L and lists the 2020 RX as receiving standard 8-inch and optional 12.3-inch touchscreen systems. [1] The linked manufacturer specification PDF was discoverable as a direct URL but did not yield extractable text through the fetch tool; therefore, its contents should be downloaded and checked manually before quoting any number from it.

The accessible 2020 RX brochure reproduction identifies the standard RX (as opposed to RXL) as a two-row vehicle with a 40/20/40-split folding, reclining second-row seat. It separately identifies RX 350L as adding a power-folding/reclining 50/50-split third-row seat. The same brochure states that RX 350 AWD is an offered configuration with a 3.5-liter V6 and 8-speed electronically controlled automatic transmission. This supports the five-seat-vs-three-row distinction and AWD identity, but the host is a dealer brochure repository rather than Lexus itself. Treat it as **C**, not as a primary commercial-rights grant. [2]

The same brochure’s dimensions block visibly states overall length 192.5 in for RX/RXh, wheelbase 109.8 in, width 74.6 in with mirrors folded, and height 67.7 in for RX/RXh/RXhL (with a separate 67.3-in RXL height). These are brochure-stated exterior dimensions, not measured interior-cabin dimensions. Do not substitute them for headroom, legroom, shoulder room, hip room, cargo opening, seat hardpoints, or any other interior dimension. [2]

The Toyota-hosted PDF at `OM48566U` is an accessible Lexus owner’s manual containing detailed seat, rear-seat, head-restraint, belt, SRS, and control-operation material. Its foreword says it applies to all models and equipment, including options, so it is useful for operation and safety-system structure but is not a trim-specific parts drawing and does not, on the inspected pages, establish a unique 2020 RX 350 AWD VIN/model code. It should be labeled “Lexus owner manual; broad model applicability; exact year/trim not established from visible foreword.” [3]

The OEM catalog pages provide useful component taxonomy and fitment evidence. The Lexus OEM Parts Online 2020 RX350 base 3.5L V6 page exposes separate center-console diagrams named “Console Body,” “Shifter Assembly,” “Left,” and “Courtesy Lamp,” and lists console-body variants with color descriptions such as black/red and black/ocher. The page URL encodes 2020 Lexus RX350, base, 3.5L V6 gas; its page content does not independently prove AWD for every listed part, so use it for catalog structure and component naming, not for universal AWD interchangeability. [4]

The same catalog’s 2020 RX350 rear-seat page visibly enumerates headrests, seat-back covers, heaters, finish panels, recline covers, rear power-seat controls, heater controls, and harnesses. It also exposes applicability qualifiers such as “without F sport,” “without hybrid,” “with power/without power,” color, “Japan built,” “US built,” and “with captains chairs.” These qualifiers are important: a generic 2020 RX350 page contains multiple option and build variants, so a reference asset must not assume one upholstery, seat-power, market-build, or seat architecture from the page alone. [5]

The 2020 RX350 dashboard page exposes instrument-panel/cluster-bezel parts, reinforcement members, defroster nozzles with/without head-up display, side covers, and speaker-grille variants for 9-, 12-, and 15-speaker systems. This establishes a dashboard component vocabulary and documents option branching, but it is not a removal procedure or dimensional drawing. [6]

An official Lexus parts item page identifies a “Left Hand Rear Door Trim Panel Assembly,” Genuine Lexus part number 67640-06P30-C8, with color RED/RED/SILVER. Its URL path includes `Lexus_2020_RX-350`, and the page describes interior coverage and model-matched fit. The catalog page also says fit should be checked and that dealer inventory/installation conditions apply. This is strong evidence for a 2020 RX 350 interior part identity, but the visible page does not establish that this exact color/part is standard on every AWD five-seat trim. [7]

## Service/removal information and access limits

Toyota’s Technical Information System (TIS) landing page states that TIS is the service-support source for Toyota-produced vehicles marketed in North America, covering most 1990-and-later products. It lists Repair Manuals and Wiring Diagrams as TIS-library features and says Emergency Responder and Dismantler Information is available free through the Manuals tab. The page is login/subscription-oriented and shows paid account pathways. [8]

Lexus support states that many Lexus reference materials, including Repair Manuals and Wiring Diagrams, may be obtained through a subscription to TIS; it also directs users to Lexus dealers or Helm for publications. This confirms that factory removal/installation procedures are not generally exposed as an open, freely inspectable Lexus web page. [9] A search result for a ClubLexus-hosted 2016–2020 RX350 repair-manual PDF and user-uploaded video pages was not treated as primary service evidence: the former is an unauthorized-looking mirror/user attachment, and the latter pages expose titles/snippets but were not inspected as technical footage. No video footage is claimed to have been reviewed.

For the reference package, cite TIS as an access/provenance lead, not as evidence that a particular 2020 RX 350 AWD trim removal sequence was reviewed. The exact repair operation, fastener locations, torque values, clip sequence, and connector cautions remain unverified unless an authorized TIS subscription or a legitimately supplied factory manual is available.

## Model, market, and applicability cautions

The target should be recorded as **2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD, standard RX two-row/five-seat body, U.S. market**. Do not merge it with RX 350L: the brochure explicitly separates RX and RXL and assigns the third row to RXL. Do not merge gasoline RX 350 parts with RX 450h/450hL or with F SPORT without checking the catalog’s qualifiers. The OEM pages show build and option distinctions including hybrid/non-hybrid, F SPORT/non-F SPORT, power/non-power rear seating, upholstery color, speaker system, HUD, and Japan-built/US-built variants. [2] [5] [6]

No exact Toyota/Lexus internal model code (for example, a full frame/model-code string) was established from the inspected public pages. The catalog URL’s “2020 Lexus RX350 / base / 3.5L V6 gas” is a catalog selection label, not a substitute for a VIN-derived model code. Do not invent or normalize a model code from it.

## Rights and provenance status

Public accessibility is not commercial permission. The official Lexus pressroom page carries a copyright notice stating that its materials are for editorial use and that commercial advertising/marketing use is prohibited; it also says downloaded materials are subject to the displayed terms. [1] The manufacturer-hosted owner manual and specification links are evidence sources, but copying pages, diagrams, photographs, or manual illustrations into a commercial AutoLab package requires separate rights review. OEM catalog pages are useful for identification and fitment research; they do not grant a license to reproduce catalog imagery or proprietary diagrams. The dealer brochure host is a secondary reproduction and should not be treated as a Lexus license. TIS material is subscription-controlled and should not be redistributed without authorization.
