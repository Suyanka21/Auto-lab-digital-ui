# Video research memo — 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD five-seat interior

**Research date:** 2026-11-16  
**Scope:** Public video pages searched for walkarounds, ownership/tutorial tours, teardown, door/seat/headliner work, and salvage footage relevant to the 2020 Lexus RX 350 AWD five-seat interior. This memo records only details supported by an opened page or accessible transcript. Public accessibility is not treated as commercial permission.

## Evidence key used here

- **A:** Opened source with a model-year/vehicle identification and transcript or page text that directly supports the interior observation or procedure.
- **B:** Opened source with useful direct transcript/page evidence, but trim, market, exact vehicle configuration, or applicability is incomplete.
- **C:** Opened page is accessible only at title/description/music/metadata level; it is not sufficient to claim that specific interior footage was inspected.
- **D:** Related vehicle/year or broad family source; useful context only, not a 2020 five-seat RX 350 identity source.

## Findings from inspectable sources

The clearest five-seat reference is the MotorMouth 2020 RX350 review. Its transcript identifies the 2020 Lexus RX350 and later identifies the shown vehicle as an F SPORT. At approximately **03:05–03:08**, the presenters describe the interior as having stitching and a distinctive flat-surface/dashboard treatment. At approximately **03:08–03:15**, they say the back seat is roomy. At approximately **03:20–03:28**, they characterize the vehicle as a midsize utility vehicle with **seating for five**, while distinguishing the optional three-row 5+2 variant. At approximately **03:45–03:50**, they state that heated steering wheel and heated/ventilated seats are standard on the vehicle being discussed. These are transcript-supported observations, not measurements. [1]

The North Park Lexus dealer walkaround is a particularly useful direct cabin reference. Its accessible transcript identifies a **newly refreshed 2020 Lexus RX350**, atomic-silver exterior, parchment interior, and bamboo wood trim. It states that the shown vehicle has automatic locks/windows, folding mirrors, and three-person memory seating. At the transcript’s opening interior section, it describes an 8-inch display, Lexus remote touchpad plus touchscreen, CD/radio controls, climate controls, USB charging ports, phone holder, heated and ventilated driver/passenger seats, and drive-mode controls. It also describes a HomeLink system, Safety Connect, and a tilt/slide sunroof. In the rear-cabin section, it identifies rear A/C vents, two additional rear power outlets, a two-cupholder center armrest, and a total of six USB charging ports. The transcript does not explicitly say AWD, exact package name, or whether the vehicle is the two-row five-seat body rather than the available three-row family; do not generalize every listed feature to every RX 350. [2]

The official Lexus 2020 RX/F SPORT walkaround is useful for trim variation. Its transcript identifies the 2020 RX family and states that active-torque-control all-wheel drive is available. For the F SPORT, it describes an optional circuit-red interior, aluminum trim, F SPORT steering wheel, gauge cluster, shift knob, pedals, and scuff plates. It also describes high-bolstered F SPORT seats and says the RX family includes two-row and three-row choices. This supports the existence of visible trim differences, but it is a promotional family presentation rather than an inspection of one specific five-seat AWD vehicle. [3]

The Lexus technology specialist tutorial is an opened, transcript-backed 2020 RX tutorial. The page says the presenter is showing one 2020 RX and cautions that equipment may differ. The accessible transcript identifies a two-row version when discussing the rear cargo area and says the rear seats fold via a lever. It also describes seat-side adjustment controls, power tilt/telescoping steering wheel, three-position driver memory, mirror controls, moonroof controls, dome lights, touchscreen/remote touchpad, climate controls, and an “S-flow” setting that can direct air to the front seats or all seating areas. The extraction contains no stable timestamps for these passages, so timestamps are not invented here. The source is valuable for control locations and operation concepts, but the presenter’s explicit equipment caveat means it cannot establish that every feature is fitted to every 2020 RX 350 AWD. [4]

The Next Step Tech Tips video is the strongest teardown/repair source found. Its transcript explicitly identifies a **2020 Lexus RX 350** and describes rear-door trim-panel and outside-door-handle removal. At **00:04–00:14**, it states the scope. At **00:14–00:21**, it identifies the access plate behind the interior door handle and carpet trim in the door grab. At **01:14–02:11**, it describes removing the rear window switch, the screw beneath it, and prying the trim panel from the bottom and around the sides. At **02:14–02:35**, it states that the door-handle cable, trim-panel wiring harness, and door-lock cable must be disconnected. At **02:38–03:13**, it describes removing the water shield and accessing the door-handle wiring. At **04:18–05:55**, it covers the connector, latch assembly, T30 Torx fastener, end cap, and sliding/pulling the exterior handle from the door. This is procedural evidence for panel/handle construction and access sequence; it is not a claim about hidden fastener count beyond what the transcript says, and it does not establish seat, headliner, or AWD details. [5]

A separate opened page titled “Lexus rx350 2020 Door panel sweep removal” exposes the title, uploader, date, duration, and related-video metadata but no usable transcript or inspectable procedure in the extracted page. It should be treated as a lead only, not evidence that the footage was reviewed. [6]

The 2016–2022 Lexus RX 350 A-pillar tutorial has an accessible transcript and explicitly presents itself as an RX A-pillar-removal demonstration applicable to the 2016–2022 range. It describes turning a locking retainer, pulling the pillar trim, and reversing the alignment/locking steps, with timestamps from **00:00–01:50**. Because the page does not identify a particular 2020 vehicle or trim, it is a related-range procedural reference rather than direct 2020 evidence. It may help frame A-pillar access near the headliner, but it does not establish 2020 headliner construction or a complete headliner-removal method. [7]

A 2020 Lexus RX 350 dealer inventory page was opened. The page identifies the vehicle as a 2020 Lexus RX 350 and its description claims AWD, leather seats, heated/cooled seats, leather-wrapped steering wheel, leatherette door-trim inserts, sunroof/moonroof, and technology features. The accessible page is primarily a short inventory video/listing and does not provide an inspectable interior transcript or stable frame-by-frame evidence. Treat these as seller-described listing attributes, not independent visual confirmation. [8]
